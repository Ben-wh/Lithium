Welcome to Lithium! This is where you ask for help and follow your requests through to the end. This guide covers everything you can do.

## Getting around

The bar at the top of every page has:

- **Dashboard**: a summary of your requests and your active tickets.
- **Tickets**: every ticket you've raised.
- **Knowledge base**: guides and answers to common questions, like this one.
- **New ticket**: raise a new request from anywhere.
- **The bell**: your notifications.
- **Your picture**: your profile, the theme (light, dark or match your system) and **Sign out**.

## Before you raise a ticket

- **Search the knowledge base first.** Your question may already have an answer.
- **Check whether it's just you.** If colleagues have the same problem, say so: it helps the team spot bigger issues.
- **Restart the app or device.** It fixes more than you'd think.

## Raising a ticket

Click **New ticket** and fill in:

1. **Subject**: short and specific. "Can't print from Outlook on the 3rd floor" beats "Printer broken".
2. **Category**: pick the one that fits best, so your ticket reaches the right people. Some categories ask a few extra questions to save back-and-forth later.
3. **Description**: what happened, what you expected instead, when it started and anything that changed around then. Copy any error message as text.
4. **Priority**: see the table below.
5. **Images**: if your workspace allows them, attach up to five screenshots or photos. You can drag them in, choose them, or paste a screenshot straight onto the page.

Press **Raise ticket** (or Ctrl+Enter, ⌘+Enter on a Mac) to send it.

| Priority | Use it when… |
| --- | --- |
| **Low** | It's minor and there's no rush |
| **Normal** | It needs doing but isn't stopping your work |
| **High** | It's blocking some of your work |
| **Urgent** | Something critical is down for you or others |

> **Tip:** please keep **Urgent** for real emergencies, so they stand out.

## Following your tickets

Every ticket has a reference like **TID-42**. Open **Tickets** to see yours. The tabs filter by status, and the search box finds tickets by subject or reference.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Unassigned** | Waiting for someone to pick it up |
| **In progress** | Someone's working on it |
| **Pending** | Waiting on you or someone else. Check for a reply |
| **Resolved** | Fixed, waiting for you to confirm |
| **Closed** | Done, no further action |

Open a ticket to see its details, who's looking after it, and the conversation. Write in the reply box at the bottom and press **Reply** (or Ctrl/⌘+Enter) to answer the team. If your workspace allows it, type **@** to mention someone on the ticket and notify them.

If a resolved ticket isn't actually fixed, reply to say so and the team will pick it back up.

## Notifications

The bell at the top shows a count when something happens on your tickets: a reply, a mention, or a change of status or priority. Open it to jump to the ticket, or **Mark all as read**.

If email is set up, you'll get emails too. Several updates to a ticket within a couple of minutes arrive as one email. Emails link to the ticket but never include the reply itself, so open Lithium to read it.

## Your profile

Open your picture at the top right, then your profile, to:

- **Choose a profile picture**: one of the built-in avatars, or upload your own (if allowed).
- **Choose which emails you get**: replies, mentions, and status changes, or turn emails off. You'll still see everything in the app.
- **Change your password**. You stay signed in on this device; your other devices are signed out.
- **Turn on two-factor authentication**: see below.

## Keeping your account safe

Two-factor authentication asks for a code from an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password) as well as your password. To set it up, choose **Set up** under **Two-factor authentication** on your profile and scan the QR code.

You'll get **10 recovery codes**. Keep them somewhere safe: each one signs you in once if you lose your phone. Generate new ones from your profile when you're running low. Your workspace may require two-factor, in which case you'll be asked to set it up when you sign in.

**Forgot your password?** Choose **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and enter your email. An administrator will check it's really you and give you a new one, and you'll choose your own the next time you sign in.
